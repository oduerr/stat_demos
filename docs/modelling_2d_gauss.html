The 2-D Gaussian — modelling correlated data

An interactive port of Modelling2D_Gauss.R. Drag the sliders for the centres $\mu_1,\mu_2$, the spreads $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$ and the correlation $\rho$. Each change draws $N=1000$ fresh samples (blue rings = the 30 %, 60 %, 80 % probability contours).

1 · Correlated data

  • Variables come in correlated pairs: attack / defence, intercept / slope.
  • Build that correlation into the prior → bivariate Gaussian $\binom{x_1}{x_2}\sim\mathcal N(\boldsymbol\mu,\mathbf K)$.

2 · Five parameters

  • $\mu_1,\mu_2$ — centre (offset).
  • $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$ — spread per axis.
  • $\rho\in[-1,1]$ — correlation; $\rho\to\pm1$ collapses the cloud onto a line.

3 · The density

$$p(\mathbf x)=\frac{1}{2\pi\,\sqrt{\det\mathbf K}}\; \exp\!\Big(-\tfrac12\,(\mathbf x-\boldsymbol\mu)^{\!\top}\,\mathbf K^{-1}\,(\mathbf x-\boldsymbol\mu)\Big)$$
$\mathbf K=$ 1.000.000.001.00 $\det\mathbf K=$ 1.00

4 · Decomposition $\mathbf K=\mathbf S\,\mathbf R\,\mathbf S$

$$\mathbf K=\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}\sigma_1&0\\[2pt]0&\sigma_2\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf S} \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1&\rho\\[2pt]\rho&1\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf R} \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}\sigma_1&0\\[2pt]0&\sigma_2\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf S}$$
1.000.000.001.00 · 1.000.000.001.00 · 1.000.000.001.00 = 1.000.000.001.00

5 · Sampling

$$\mathbf x=\boldsymbol\mu+\mathbf L\,\mathbf z,\quad \mathbf L\mathbf L^{\!\top}=\mathbf K,\quad \mathbf z\sim\mathcal N(\mathbf 0,\mathbf I)$$

i.e. R's mvrnorm(N, mu, Sigma=K). Empirical sd's and correlation below match the sliders (up to noise).
