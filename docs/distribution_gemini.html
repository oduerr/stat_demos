Distribution Playground
Expectation
Shows the expectation (mean) as the center of mass of the distribution, and the standard deviation as its spread.
Move the parameters to see how the balance point and variability change.
Probability (CDF)
Shows the probability that the random variable is ≤ x, using the CDF.
Drag the triangle to pick an x-value.
Note: For continuous variables, interval probabilities are computed as F(b) − F(a).
Quantiles
Shows which x-value corresponds to a chosen probability p.
Drag the probability slider to see the quantile x = F⁻¹(p).
Useful for medians, percentiles, and thresholds.
