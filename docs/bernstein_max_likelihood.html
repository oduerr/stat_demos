Bernstein Maximum Likelihood App

Normalizing Flow with Bernstein Polynomials

Goal: Transform a simple Standard Normal Distribution \( z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1) \) (Latent Space) into a complex distribution that fits the observed data \( x \) (Data Space).
Model: \( f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{M} \vartheta_k B_{k}^{M}(x) \). We learn the coefficients \( \vartheta_k \) to maximize the likelihood.
Loss (NLL): \( \mathcal{L} = - \sum \left( \log p_z(f(x)) + \log |f'(x)| \right) \).
