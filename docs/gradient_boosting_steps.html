Gradient boosting, step by step

The model f(x) sets the parameter of a distribution, the loss is the NLL. Each stage asks every training point in which direction its prediction should move (the negative gradient of its loss), fits a tree to these answers, sets the values in its leaves with the loss and adds the tree to the model. Change the distribution: only the answers of the points change. Click a point to see its own loss curve.
