PDF → CDF as Integration

Manim animation: cumulative probability mass builds the CDF
← Back to STAT Demos

This animation visualizes the construction of the cumulative distribution function from a probability density function.

The upper panel shows the probability density function \(f(x)\). Each colored bin represents a small probability mass between \(x_i\) and \(x_{i+1}\).

In the lower panel, these masses are accumulated to form the cumulative distribution function \(F(x)\). At each position, all previously added contributions remain visible, emphasizing that \(F(x)\) includes the total probability mass of all values \(t \le x\).

In the limit of infinitesimally small bins, we obtain the following relationship between the PDF and the CDF: \[ F(x) = \int_{-\infty}^{x} f(t)\,dt . \]
