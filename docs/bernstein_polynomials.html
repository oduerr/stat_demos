Bernstein Polynomials Explorer

Interactive visualization of Bernstein basis functions

The Bernstein polynomial of degree \(M\) is defined as: $$ g(z) = \sum_{k=0}^{M} \vartheta_k B_{k}^{M}(z) $$ where the basis functions are: $$ B_{k}^{M}(z) = \binom{M}{k} z^k (1 - z)^{M-k} $$

Key Insight: Bernstein polynomials are a partition of unity, meaning \(\sum B_k^M(z) = 1\). They form the mathematical basis for Bézier curves used in computer graphics.
